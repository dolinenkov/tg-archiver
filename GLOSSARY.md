# Telegram Archive

A personal archive of Telegram conversations and their content, retained independently of later changes in Telegram.

## Language

**Account**:
The single personal Telegram account whose accessible conversations are archived.

**Chat**:
A private conversation, group, or channel accessible to the account. Chats are included by default unless explicitly excluded.

**Archive**:
The locally readable and searchable collection of saved messages and content, browsable through a web interface. Saved content survives deletion in Telegram.
_Avoid_: Mirror

**Message version**:
A saved observation of a message's content, either its initial captured state or an observed edit. Earlier saved versions remain in the archive.

**Attachment**:
Content associated with a message, such as a photo, video, voice message, or file. Attachment categories can be excluded from collection.
